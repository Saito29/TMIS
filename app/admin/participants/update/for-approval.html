About Review Workflow

Each farmer profile progresses through these four steps.

For ReviewFor ApprovalApprovedAccepted
For Review
The farmer profile has been submitted and is awaiting verification of encoded information and supporting details.
For Approval
The profile has completed initial review and is forwarded to the appropriate approval process.
Approved
The farmer profile has successfully completed review and approval and is ready for participant acceptance.
Accepted
The approved farmer profile has been accepted and is finalized in the system.
