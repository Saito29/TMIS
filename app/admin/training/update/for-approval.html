About Approval Workflow

Each training progresses through these three steps.

For ReviewFor ApprovalApproved
For Review
The training information and supporting design have passed review and are awaiting the approving officer's decision.
For Approval
The training has passed review and is awaiting approval. The approving officer can approve it or return it with remarks.
Approved
The training has completed the approval process and is finalized in the system.
